A QUICK PRIVACY CHECK

Your data, your call

SpeakUp sends your chat text to Google Gemini to get AI replies and English corrections. If you turn pronunciation feedback on, the app also sends the short audio clip from that turn for approximate guidance.

Practice notes and summaries are saved in this browser only. Recordings are used for that AI request and are not saved by SpeakUp. Google’s free-tier terms may allow submitted content to be used to improve products, so don’t practise with private or sensitive details.

Your API key is stored in this browser and passed through the app’s server to Google. Keep the app and device private; don’t share a public link if you add a server-side key.
